HOW THIS WORKS

1. Field agents photograph polling-unit result sheets (EC8A) with tamper-evident hashing.
2. Two-person verification reviews documents against IReV observations.
3. Verified figures are published here with source, status and timestamp.
4. Every published number traces back to a polling-unit record.
5. Discrepancies are reported factually: "RESULT PREVIOUSLY OBSERVED — CURRENTLY NOT OBSERVED" — never speculation.
